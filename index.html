Workflow 01

💥 Nuke Your Likes

Prune your account for a fresh algorithm by clearing your X likes.

  1. Navigate to https://x.com/YOUR_USERNAME/likes and replace YOUR_USERNAME.
  2. Open your browser's Developer Tools.
  3. Open the Console, paste the script below, and press Enter.
  4. Keep the tab open and visible until the script finishes.
Note: The default configuration uses a jittered 850–1,300 ms delay and pauses for 9 seconds after every 45 unlikes. X may still throttle large batches.

Workflow 02

👋 Nuke Your Unfollowers

Clean up your Following list by unfollowing accounts that do not follow you back. Anyone marked Follows you is skipped.

  1. Navigate to https://x.com/YOUR_USERNAME/following and replace YOUR_USERNAME.
  2. Open your browser's Developer Tools.
  3. Open the Console, paste the script below, and press Enter.
  4. Keep the tab open and visible until the script finishes.
Note: The script stops after 2,500 unfollows and targets one unfollow per second. Increase the action interval if X begins rate limiting the account.

  1. Navigate to https://x.com/YOUR_USERNAME/following and replace YOUR_USERNAME.
  2. Open your browser's Developer Tools.
  3. Open the Console, paste the script below, and press Enter.
  4. Keep the tab open and visible until the script finishes.
Review the keyword list first: Broad terms such as link in bio, 18+, and Instagram references can match unrelated accounts. By default, the script can also unfollow accounts that follow you back, waits 6–14 seconds between actions, and stops after 60 unfollows.
